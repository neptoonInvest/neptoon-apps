Why doesn't Neptoon ask for my salary?

Neptoon is not a household budgeting tool. You therefore do not need to enter your salary, income taxes and ordinary living expenses separately. What matters is not how much you earn, but how much of your disposable income you choose to allocate between consumption and savings each month. The monthly savings contribution is invested in your selected Free Investment Portfolio during Phase 1. If you have outstanding debt, your monthly contribution must exceed the interest charged for that contribution to increase your wealth. Any amount above the interest expense increases your wealth, including the portion used to repay loan principal. You may also choose to consume all your disposable income and set your monthly contribution to zero, even if you have debt. The model will then attempt to finance your debt obligations using other economic resources available throughout your life. This is the central idea of a Life Cycle model: allocating resources across time. Neptoon warns you if your financial objectives cannot be sustained under the selected assumptions.

Does Neptoon assume my income remains constant throughout my life?

No, but the model uses a simplified representation of your future savings. You enter one monthly savings contribution and one fixed annual growth rate, which may, for example, reflect expected salary growth. This creates a consistent savings trajectory but does not automatically capture major or irregular changes in your income. You can approximate such changes through My Settings.

What if my income increases in the future?

If you expect to be able to invest more over time, you can select a positive annual growth rate for your monthly savings. For example, a monthly contribution of DKK 4,000 growing by 3% annually will increase to approximately DKK 5,400 after ten years. This growth rate applies to your savings contributions, not to the investment returns earned on your portfolio.

I am a student and expect a significantly higher income later. What should I do?

You can enter your current monthly savings contribution, potentially DKK 0, and then use Other Future Values to represent the additional savings you expect after completing your studies. You calculate a total future amount that approximates the additional investments over a chosen period and specify the age at which the amount should enter the model. This is a simplified approach rather than a detailed monthly income forecast.

Can I model a major salary increase or career change?

Yes, approximately. For example, if you expect to invest an additional DKK 5,000 per month for ten years following a career change, this corresponds to DKK 600,000 before any investment returns. You can use Other Future Values in My Settings to represent these additional savings as a synthetic lump sum. The amount and timing should be chosen carefully, because a single future payment does not produce exactly the same financial trajectory as recurring monthly contributions. You can also download the Excel workbook and enter more flexible amounts.

What if my income decreases or I take an extended break from work?

An expected gradual reduction in your savings can be reflected through a lower or negative annual growth rate. Larger temporary changes, such as parental leave, further education or unemployment, cannot be modeled as separate monthly income periods. Instead, you can adjust your average savings trajectory or use a synthetic future value if the overall financial impact can reasonably be represented in that way.

What does a synthetic income stream mean under Other Future Values?

It means converting an expected future change in your disposable savings into a single amount. Instead of entering, for example, 60 additional monthly contributions, you can calculate their combined economic value and register it as a future value. Neptoon includes this value in your economic Balance Sheet and transfers it to your Free Investment Portfolio at the selected date. This method is an approximation and is particularly useful for substantial, identifiable changes in future income.

Should I include public pension and ATP as income or savings?

Public pension and ATP Lifelong Pension should be entered separately under Public Pension and ATP. They should not be added to your monthly savings contributions or registered as synthetic future values. Neptoon automatically includes the pension payments and their economic value, ensuring that the same resources are not counted more than once.

What happens to my salary when I reach Economic Balance Day and enter the consumption phase?

Economic Balance Day is the earliest point at which your remaining economic resources can, according to the model, finance your desired consumption for the rest of your life while also meeting your Legacy objective, without requiring further employment income. Technically, this means without additional monthly savings contributions. Neptoon therefore assumes that regular savings contributions stop and the Life Cycle transitions to Phase 2. You may naturally continue working in real life, but any subsequent employment income is not automatically included in the calculation.

Hvorfor fortsætter min formue med at vokse efter min økonomiske frihedsdag?

Økonomisk frihed betyder ikke nødvendigvis, at formuen skal falde. I forbrugsfasen kan investeringernes afkast overstige dit løbende forbrug. I så fald fortsætter den økonomiske formue med at vokse, selvom du allerede er økonomisk uafhængig.

Hvorfor kan min forventede arv overstige mit mål for arv?

Dit mål for arv er en minimumsbetingelse for formuen ved planlægningshorisontens afslutning, ikke et maksimum. Når din økonomiske frihedsdag allerede er i dag, kan modellen ikke flytte frihedstidspunktet længere tilbage. Hvis dine økonomiske ressourcer overstiger det nødvendige til forbrug og målsat arv, bliver overskuddet en del af den forventede arv.

Kan jeg bruge den overskydende arv til et højere forbrug?

Ja. Du kan øge dit ønskede månedlige forbrug eller ændre dit mål for arv. Neptoon Life Cycle genberegner derefter den tidligst mulige økonomiske frihedsdag. Modellen øger ikke automatisk dit forbrug og tvinger heller ikke den forventede arv til at svare præcist til målet. Det er dine egne økonomiske prioriteringer.
