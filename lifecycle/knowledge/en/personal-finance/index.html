What is a Life Cycle model?

A Life Cycle model explores the relationship between your finances today and your opportunities throughout the rest of your life. Rather than focusing solely on your current savings, Neptoon also considers future pension income, the value of your home and business, outstanding debt, expected inheritance and other economic resources. The model brings these elements together into one financial life cycle and illustrates how they can finance your desired standard of living and Legacy.

What is the fundamental idea behind Neptoon?

The fundamental idea is that economic resources can be allocated across time. Wealth that will not become available for another 10 or 20 years may already have economic significance for your decisions today. Neptoon therefore examines not only how much wealth you have, but also when your resources become available, how they develop and how they can finance your future consumption. The model connects saving, investing, financing and consumption in one integrated economic calculation.

Is Neptoon Life Cycle an artificial intelligence (AI) model?

No. Neptoon Life Cycle is not an AI engine. Its financial calculations are based on classical economic principles, particularly intertemporal household economics, lifetime resource allocation and consumption optimization. The model applies a deterministic and stochastic mathematical framework to evaluate how a household's economic resources can finance consumption and a desired Legacy throughout life. Given the same inputs and assumptions, the model produces the same results. Artificial intelligence has played an important role in developing the software infrastructure, programming and user interface, but AI does not generate, predict or determine the financial calculations. The economic methodology and modeling assumptions are defined by Neptoon Wealth.

Who developed Neptoon Life Cycle?

Neptoon Life Cycle was developed by Thomas Peter Clausen, a Danish economist and author with 20 years of experience in international banking. The model draws on classical economic theory, particularly intertemporal household economics, lifetime resource allocation and the optimization of financial freedom. Thomas Peter Clausen is responsible for the economic methodology and underlying modeling principles. Artificial intelligence has contributed significantly to the development of the software infrastructure, but the financial calculations are based on deterministic economic life cycle model with stochastic overlay rather than AI-generated predictions. Learn more about the founder and Neptoon Wealth at www.neptoonwealth.com.

What is the difference between economic wealth and traditional accounting wealth?

A traditional statement of wealth typically shows the value of your current assets minus your liabilities. Neptoon's economic Balance Sheet goes further by also including the present value of expected future income, such as public pension and ATP. The principle is similar to equity valuation, where analysts consider not only a company's existing assets but also the value of its expected future earnings. Economic wealth is therefore a broader concept than the wealth you can withdraw or sell today.

Can my home finance my consumption even if I sell it much later?

Yes, within Neptoon's economic model. Imagine that you have substantial equity in your home but do not intend to sell it for another 15 years. The model can incorporate this future economic resource into the calculation of your overall financial life cycle. If financing is needed before the sale, the Free Investment Portfolio may temporarily have a negative balance, which accrues interest at the model's selected financing rate. When the property is eventually sold, the associated mortgage is repaid and the net proceeds are transferred to the Free Investment Portfolio. This is an economic modeling assumption, not a guarantee that a bank will provide equivalent financing.

Does financial freedom mean I have to sell my home or stop working?

No. Neptoon calculates the earliest point at which your total economic resources can finance your desired consumption and Legacy under the selected assumptions. This does not mean that you must stop working, sell your home or change your lifestyle. Financial Freedom Day is a calculated economic outcome, not a recommendation to make a particular life decision.

Does Neptoon decide how much I should consume or save?

No. You decide your monthly savings, your desired future consumption and your Legacy. Neptoon does not optimize your personal preferences or choose your financial objectives. Instead, the model calculates when your chosen objectives can be financed under the specified assumptions. You can then adjust your choices and explore the consequences.

Where do Neptoon's investment models come from?

Neptoon develops its own investment models and comparison portfolios using instruments such as publicly traded investment funds, ETFs, stocks and bonds. Some portfolios are constructed to illustrate investment profiles offered by major European banks, while others are based on Neptoon's own strategic and tactical investment principles. Neptoon independently estimates the portfolios' expected returns, risks and costs using its modeling assumptions. Bank-related portfolios are Neptoon's own representations and do not necessarily reflect the banks' official recommendations or products.

Does Neptoon automatically choose my investments?

No. You choose the investment portfolio used during the wealth-building phase and the portfolio used during the consumption phase. Neptoon applies the selected portfolios' expected after-tax returns in the Life Cycle calculation. The model does not automatically optimize your portfolio, trade securities or change your investments on your behalf.

Hvad betyder de to flag, og hvorfor ændrer mine økonomiske resultater sig, når jeg skifter?

De to flag i Neptoon Life Cycle repræsenterer to forskellige økonomiske husholdningsprofiler – ikke blot et valg af sprog. Det danske flag vælger dansk sprog, DKK og danske standardforudsætninger, herunder Folkepension og ATP. Det britiske flag vælger den internationale profil med engelsk sprog, EUR og neutrale pensionsforudsætninger uden de særlige danske pensionsbeløb. Derfor kan din beregnede økonomiske formue og tidspunktet for økonomisk frihed ændre sig, når du skifter profil. Det skyldes ændrede modelforudsætninger – ikke at din faktiske formue eller dine pensionsrettigheder ændrer sig. Begge profiler kan tilpasses din personlige økonomi. Under 'Mine Indstillinger' kan du selv ændre pensionsbeløb, pensionsalder og øvrige økonomiske forudsætninger, så beregningerne afspejler din egen situation.

Why does my wealth increase and Danish pensions such as Folkepension and ATP appear when I switch language?

The two flags in Neptoon Life Cycle represent different household profiles, not simply different languages. The Danish profile uses DKK and includes Danish pension assumptions, including Folkepension (the Danish public pension) and ATP (the supplementary public pension). When you switch from the international profile to the Danish profile, these pension amounts may be initialized with Danish default values. Neptoon includes the estimated present value of future pension payments in its economic Balance Sheet, which can substantially increase your calculated economic wealth. This does not mean that changing language creates actual wealth or pension entitlements. It means that the model is applying a different set of household assumptions. Select the profile that best reflects your economic circumstances and adjust the pension amounts to your actual entitlements.

What happens if I am already financially free today?

If your current economic wealth is sufficient to finance your chosen lifetime consumption and target Legacy, Neptoon Life Cycle sets Financial Freedom Day to today (n* = 0). The Building Wealth phase has zero duration, and the model immediately applies the Using Wealth phase until the end of your planning horizon.

Why does my wealth continue growing after Financial Freedom Day?

Financial freedom does not necessarily imply declining wealth. During the Using Wealth phase, your investments may generate returns exceeding your consumption needs. In that case, economic wealth continues growing even though you are already financially independent.

Why can my projected Legacy exceed my target Legacy?

Your target Legacy is a minimum terminal wealth objective, not a maximum. When Financial Freedom Day is today, the model cannot move the freedom date any earlier. If your resources exceed the amount required to finance consumption and the target Legacy, the remaining surplus appears as additional projected Legacy.

Can I use the surplus Legacy for higher consumption instead?

Yes. You can increase your monthly consumption target or adjust your Legacy objective. Neptoon Life Cycle then recalculates the earliest feasible Financial Freedom Day. The model does not automatically increase consumption or force Legacy to equal its target: these remain your personal financial choices.

Can I use the surplus Legacy for higher consumption instead?

Yes. When Financial Freedom Day is already today (n* = 0), a substantial surplus above your target Legacy means that you may be able to finance significantly higher monthly consumption throughout the Using Wealth phase. Increasing consumption reduces the projected terminal Legacy, allowing you to enjoy more of your wealth during your lifetime. As long as the target Legacy remains achievable, Financial Freedom Day stays at today. Neptoon Life Cycle lets you explore this trade-off without automatically changing your personal objectives.

Why is my net wealth in Neptoon higher than the wealth I normally calculate?

Neptoon Life Cycle measures your economic net wealth rather than only your current assets minus liabilities. The model also includes the present value of future economic resources, such as public and supplementary pension payments. Your economic net wealth may therefore be substantially higher than the wealth shown in a conventional personal balance sheet. This does not mean that the entire amount is available as cash today.

What does Neptoon's wealth calculation have in common with stock valuation?

Imagine your household were a publicly listed company – Household Inc. An equity analyst would not value the company solely by looking at its book equity. The analyst would also consider the present value of expected future cash flows. This is a fundamental principle of equity valuation, including Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) analysis. Neptoon applies the same broad economic reasoning to a household's lifetime resources and obligations. The result is a model-based economic valuation, not an observable stock-market price for your household.

What does net equity represent in Neptoon's economic Balance Sheet?

Neptoon Life Cycle recognizes the household's current assets and liabilities together with the present value of relevant future economic resources, such as public and supplementary pensions. Two future items are deliberately excluded from recognized liabilities: planned future consumption and the target Legacy. Consequently, net equity – assets minus liabilities – represents, at any point in time, the economic reserve available to finance remaining consumption and Legacy during the second phase of life (Phase 2). The principle is comparable to the valuation of a publicly listed company: its economic value reflects assets, obligations and future cash flows, while shareholders' intended future use of that value is not itself recorded as a liability. Neptoon's Life Cycle model determines when this economic reserve becomes sufficient to finance both the desired consumption and the selected Legacy.

What is the difference between book wealth and economic wealth?

A conventional balance sheet reports assets and liabilities recognized and measured at a particular point in time. Economic wealth also considers the estimated present value of relevant future cash flows. The distinction is comparable to the difference between a company's accounting equity and an equity analyst's economic valuation. These figures need not be equal because they are based on different valuation principles.

Why are my future public and supplementary pensions included in my wealth today?

Future pension payments represent economic resources that can help finance consumption later in life. Neptoon estimates their present value and includes it in the economic balance sheet. This is conceptually similar to an equity analyst assigning value today to expected future company cash flows. However, the pension's present value is not a cash balance that you can freely withdraw or invest.

Do I become wealthier when my pension payments begin?

Not simply because payments begin. In Neptoon's economic Life Cycle model, the estimated value of future pension payments is already included in your economic wealth. When a pension payment is received, part of that future economic resource is converted into liquid funds. This changes the composition of wealth but does not, by itself, create additional economic wealth. Similarly, a company does not create new value merely by converting an existing asset into cash.

Why can I choose different investments before and after Financial Freedom Day?

Because building wealth and financing consumption are two different economic situations. While accumulating wealth, your investment horizon and willingness to take risk may differ from when your assets are needed to finance ongoing consumption. Neptoon therefore allows you to select an independent investment portfolio for each phase. You determine the investment risk, while the model calculates the consequences for your financial life cycle.

Why can Neptoon show financial freedom before my retirement age?

Because Neptoon does not start from a predetermined retirement age. Instead, the model examines when your total economic resources are sufficient to finance your desired consumption throughout the rest of your life. Future pensions, housing wealth and other assets may therefore have economic significance even before they are paid out or realized. The calculated date depends on your assumptions and does not guarantee that all resources can be converted into liquidity on the modeled terms.

Can I trust Neptoon's calculations?

Neptoon uses a consistent economic model with explicit assumptions concerning expected returns, risk, taxation, inflation, financing and life expectancy. The calculations illustrate the consequences of these assumptions, but they cannot predict the future with certainty. The purpose is to give you a better foundation for understanding your financial opportunities, exploring alternatives and making your own decisions.
