Business ownership and personal wealth

Understand how business ownership and future sale proceeds affect personal wealth.

Can I include the value of my own business?

Yes. Neptoon allows you to register the value of your ownership interest in a private business as a separate asset on your economic Balance Sheet. You enter its estimated current market value and expected annual appreciation. The business is then included in your total economic wealth in much the same way as real estate. Any deferred capital gains tax is recorded separately as a liability.

How does Neptoon calculate the future value of my business?

Neptoon projects the business's value using its entered market value and selected return assumption. The business remains a separate gross asset on the Balance Sheet until the selected realization age. Any deferred tax on nominal capital appreciation accumulates separately, so the economic net value reflects both the business's value and its associated tax liability.

What happens when I sell my business?

At the selected realization age, Neptoon calculates the business's net sale proceeds after any deferred capital gains tax and associated modeled liabilities. The net proceeds are transferred to the Free Investment Portfolio, regardless of whether the sale occurs in Phase 1 or Phase 2. The business and its associated settled liabilities are simultaneously removed from the Balance Sheet. The sale therefore represents a conversion of existing economic wealth rather than a new savings contribution.

What happens if I sell my business during Phase 1?

If the business is sold before Economic Balance Day, the calculated net proceeds are transferred to the Free Investment Portfolio in Phase 1. The proceeds then contribute to further wealth accumulation and earn the expected after-tax return of your selected Phase 1 investment portfolio. Selling your business may therefore affect the timing of Economic Balance Day.

What happens if I sell my business during Phase 2?

If the business is sold after Economic Balance Day, the net proceeds are transferred to the Free Investment Portfolio in Phase 2. The proceeds then contribute to financing your desired consumption, other financial obligations and Legacy. Subsequent returns follow the investment portfolio you selected for Phase 2.

How is tax handled when I sell my business?

Neptoon applies realization-based taxation to the modeled taxable nominal capital appreciation. The business's gross value remains recorded as an asset, while the calculated deferred capital gains tax is recorded separately as a liability. Upon sale, the tax is settled through the net proceeds, and the associated deferred tax liability is removed. The tax must therefore not be deducted from net wealth twice. The model uses simplified tax assumptions and does not calculate the business's actual tax circumstances.

What if I keep my business for the rest of my life?

If you do not specify an earlier realization age, Neptoon assumes that the business remains part of your economic wealth until the end of your selected lifetime. Its remaining net value, after any deferred tax and modeled liabilities, is then included in your Legacy. The model does not assume that you must necessarily sell your business to reach Economic Balance Day.

Does Neptoon forecast my business profits?

No. Neptoon is not a business forecasting model. It does not calculate revenue, operating profits, salaries, dividends, cash flow budgets or future business transactions. The business is treated as a capital asset with an estimated market value and expected appreciation. Any personal income from the business is assumed to be handled outside the model unless explicitly reflected in other registered economic items.

Can I include multiple businesses?

Neptoon handles one private business directly as a separate asset. If you own interests in additional businesses, you can include their expected future net proceeds under Other Future Values. You enter the value after any taxes and associated liabilities, together with the expected realization age. These additional businesses are not subject to a separate detailed business valuation calculation.

Can I reach Economic Balance Day without selling my business?

Yes. Neptoon includes the business's economic net value as part of your total resources. The model may therefore calculate Economic Balance Day before the selected realization age. This relies on the model's simplified assumptions about financing across assets and liabilities. However, a business's market value does not necessarily represent immediately available liquidity, and the model does not guarantee that the business can be used as collateral or sold at its expected value.
