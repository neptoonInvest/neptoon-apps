Home equity, property and retirement

Understand property values, home equity, mortgages and future sale proceeds.

Should I include the value of my home?

Yes, if you want your home to be included in your total economic wealth. Neptoon allows you to register one primary private property directly in the model. You enter the home's estimated current market value, not just your home equity. Any mortgage debt and bank loans are registered separately under Loans. Neptoon then calculates the property's future value, outstanding debt and economic home equity.

Can I include more than one property?

Yes, but only one private property can be registered directly with separate calculations of property value development, loan financing, interest and principal repayments. If you own a holiday home, for example, you can include its expected future net proceeds under Other Future Values. You specify the expected value after any outstanding debt, taxes and selling costs, together with the age at which the value is expected to be realized. Neptoon does not separately calculate ongoing financing, operating expenses or property taxes for these additional properties.

How does Neptoon calculate my home's future value?

The property's value is projected using your selected expected real appreciation rate and the model's inflation assumption. Neptoon therefore distinguishes between real appreciation and nominal appreciation, which also includes inflation. The calculated property value is continuously included as a gross asset on your Balance Sheet, while any outstanding debt and deferred taxes are recorded separately as liabilities.

How does Neptoon handle tax deductibility on interest expense and loan costs?

System has a tax deductibility mechanism, but currently it only applies to people that logs in as Danish where the deduction is set to fixed 25% of total expense in the remaining loan period.

How does Neptoon handle property value tax and land tax?

Neptoon does not calculate Danish property value tax (ejendomsværdiskat) or land tax (grundskyld). These recurring property taxes fall outside the Life Cycle model and are assumed to be handled through the household's ordinary budget and income. They are therefore not included as separate payments through the model's central financing account, the Free Investment Portfolio. These recurring property taxes should not be confused with any capital gains tax payable when the property is sold.

Do I have to pay tax on the appreciation of my home?

By default, Neptoon applies a capital gains tax rate of 0% to the primary private residence. This reflects the general Danish principal residence exemption (parcelhusreglen), under which gains from selling an owner-occupied home are typically tax-free when the legal conditions are met. The model does not perform an individual tax assessment. If a gain is taxable, this can be reflected through the relevant tax assumptions. Any deferred tax liability is recorded separately until realization.

How does Neptoon handle interest and principal repayments?

Neptoon continuously calculates interest, principal repayments and any loan costs associated with the registered property financing. Payments are handled through the model's central financing account, the Free Investment Portfolio. Interest and financing costs reduce economic wealth, while principal repayments reduce both outstanding debt and the financial resources used to make the payments. The model does not require all loan payments to be financed directly from ongoing employment income.

Do I become wealthier by paying down my mortgage?

Not as a direct result of the repayment itself. When you repay mortgage principal, your outstanding debt decreases and your home equity increases by the same amount. At the same time, the repayment is charged to the Free Investment Portfolio. Principal repayment is therefore a reallocation between assets and liabilities and does not, by itself, create additional net wealth. Interest and financing costs, however, are economic expenses that negatively affect net wealth.

What happens when I sell my home?

At your selected realization age, Neptoon calculates the property's net sale proceeds as its future market value less the associated outstanding debt and any capital gains tax. The net proceeds are transferred to the Free Investment Portfolio, while the property and settled debt are removed from the Balance Sheet. This applies whether the sale takes place during Phase 1 – Wealth Building – or Phase 2 – Consumption. The sale is therefore a conversion of existing economic wealth rather than a new savings contribution.

What happens if I sell my home during Phase 1?

If your home is sold before Economic Balance Day, Neptoon calculates the net proceeds after settling the remaining property debt and any capital gains tax. The proceeds are transferred to the Free Investment Portfolio and subsequently develop according to the investment return selected for Phase 1. The sale can therefore affect future wealth development and the calculated Economic Balance Day.

What happens if I sell my home during Phase 2?

If your home is sold after Economic Balance Day, the net proceeds after settling outstanding property debt and any capital gains tax are transferred to the Free Investment Portfolio in Phase 2. The proceeds then contribute to financing your desired consumption, remaining financial obligations and Legacy. Subsequent investment returns follow the portfolio you selected for Phase 2, not the portfolio from the wealth-building phase.

What happens to my mortgage when my home is sold?

Neptoon assumes that the remaining registered property-related mortgage debt is settled when the home is sold. The outstanding balance is therefore deducted directly from the property's realization value before the net proceeds are transferred to the Free Investment Portfolio. At the same time, the settled debt is removed from the Balance Sheet, and its associated future loan payments cease in the model. The outstanding debt must not be deducted from the sale proceeds and then continue as a separate liability.

What if I never plan to sell my home?

If you do not specify an earlier realization age, Neptoon assumes that your home remains part of your economic wealth until the end of your selected lifetime. Its remaining net value, after associated debt and any deferred tax, is then included in your Legacy. The model may still incorporate the property's economic value into the overall Life Cycle calculation, even if the property is not sold earlier.

Can I reach Economic Balance Day without selling my home?

Yes. Neptoon includes your home's economic net value as part of the household's total resources. Your selected realization age determines when any sale proceeds are transferred to the Free Investment Portfolio. However, the model applies a simplified financing assumption under which economic resources can contribute to the overall Life Cycle calculation. Substantial home equity does not necessarily mean that equivalent cash is immediately available, and the calculation does not guarantee that a bank will provide the necessary financing.
