What does consumption mean in Neptoon Life Cycle?

Neptoon makes a fundamental distinction between consumption in Phase 1 – Wealth Building – and Phase 2 – Consumption. In Phase 1, household consumption is exogenous and is not included as a separate variable in the model. In Phase 2, however, you specify a desired fixed monthly consumption amount after tax, measured in today's purchasing power. This consumption target represents the standard of living you wish to finance through your total economic resources without depending on continued employment income.

Do I need to enter my current monthly consumption?

No. In Phase 1, Neptoon uses your monthly savings contribution rather than a detailed household budget. Your savings contribution is the amount you choose to invest after accounting for taxes and ordinary living expenses. Registered loan payments are calculated separately by Neptoon and financed through the Free Investment Portfolio. Your current consumption is therefore reflected indirectly in your savings decision and does not enter the model as an independent variable. The consumption field refers exclusively to your desired monthly consumption in Phase 2.

What exactly should I enter?

Enter the monthly amount after tax that you would like to spend on ordinary personal consumption after Economic Balance Day. For example, DKK 25,000 per month in today's purchasing power. The amount should reflect your desired standard of living but exclude interest and principal repayments on loans already registered and calculated separately by Neptoon.

Why should I exclude interest and loan repayments?

Neptoon automatically calculates future payments on your registered loans, including interest and principal repayments. These payments are accounted for separately in the Life Cycle calculation, including after Economic Balance Day. If you also include the same loan payments in your desired monthly consumption, they will be counted twice. Your consumption target should therefore exclude loan payments already handled by the model.

Is my desired consumption the same as the amount withdrawn from my investment portfolio?

No, not necessarily. Your monthly consumption is an overall economic objective, not a direct instruction to withdraw a particular amount from your portfolio. Public pension, ATP and other modeled financial inflows may contribute to financing your consumption. Neptoon therefore calculates the remaining financing requirement to be covered by your other economic resources and Free Investment Portfolio. Your desired consumption and the actual net amount drawn from the portfolio may therefore differ.

When does my desired consumption begin?

Your desired consumption begins on Economic Balance Day, when the model transitions from Phase 1 – Wealth Building – to Phase 2 – Consumption. Neptoon calculates the earliest feasible transition at which your total economic resources are expected to finance your desired consumption while also meeting your Legacy objective. From that point, modeled monthly savings contributions cease, and the Life Cycle shifts to financing consumption and remaining financial obligations.

Does my consumption increase with inflation?

Yes. Your consumption target is expressed in today's purchasing power and remains constant in real terms throughout Phase 2. Neptoon accounts for inflation so that the nominal amount adjusts over time. A consumption target of DKK 25,000 per month therefore represents a standard of living equivalent to DKK 25,000 in today's purchasing power, even many years into the future.

Can I choose a different consumption level from what I have today?

Yes. Consumption in Phase 2 is an independent economic objective and does not need to match your current consumption in Phase 1. For example, you may want a higher standard of living with more travel and leisure activities, or lower consumption with fewer fixed expenses. Neptoon uses your selected consumption target to calculate when Economic Balance Day becomes feasible.

What if my consumption changes later in life?

Neptoon applies one fixed real monthly consumption target throughout Phase 2. The model does not include a detailed consumption calendar with different spending levels at different ages. Instead, you can select a representative consumption level and test alternative amounts to explore their effects on Economic Balance Day, wealth development and Legacy.

How does my consumption affect Economic Balance Day?

Your consumption target is an exogenous assumption, while Economic Balance Day is a calculated outcome. Higher desired consumption generally requires greater economic resources and will therefore typically delay Economic Balance Day. Lower consumption may allow an earlier transition to Phase 2. Neptoon calculates the earliest feasible transition while accounting for savings, investment returns, other economic resources, loan obligations and your desired Legacy.
