Private pensions and retirement savings

Should I include my existing private pension savings?

Yes. Neptoon treats your private pension savings as a separate financial asset on your overall economic Balance Sheet. You enter the pension's current gross value, expected nominal return and any ongoing monthly pension contributions. Pension contributions are kept separate from your monthly savings contributions to the Free Investment Portfolio. The pension develops according to its own economic and tax assumptions until the selected realization age.

Is the withdrawal age of my private pension linked to the public pension age?

No. Private pension is an independent asset in Neptoon and does not automatically follow the public pension age or the timing of ATP payments. You choose the age at which your private pension is realized in the model. This may be before, at or after the public pension age. Public pension and ATP are separate income streams, while private pension is treated as an asset with its own appreciation, tax liabilities and realization age. The selected age is a modeling assumption, not an assessment of the pension scheme's actual withdrawal rules.

How does Neptoon calculate the value of my private pension?

Neptoon projects the pension asset's value based on its current value, any ongoing contributions and the selected expected nominal return after pension investment return tax. The pension asset remains recorded at its calculated gross value on the asset side of the Balance Sheet. Any deferred tax liabilities associated with future realization are recorded separately on the liability side, so the pension's economic net value reflects both the asset's value and future tax obligations.

How is PAL tax on pension investment returns handled?

Neptoon assumes that the entered nominal pension return is already net of ongoing pension investment return tax, such as Danish PAL tax. PAL tax is therefore incorporated directly into the pension asset's expected return. No additional PAL tax payment is calculated, and no separate deferred PAL tax liability accumulates. This prevents the same pension investment return from being taxed twice in the model.

Why can there be two different tax assumptions for my private pension?

Neptoon distinguishes between an existing deferred tax liability associated with today's pension wealth and calculated tax on subsequent nominal capital appreciation. Both relate to the tax settlement upon pension realization and are kept separate from ongoing PAL taxation, which is already reflected in the expected pension return. The deferred tax components are recorded as liabilities and settled at the selected realization age.

What does deferred tax on my private pension mean?

Deferred pension tax is a calculated future tax liability that has not yet been paid. Neptoon records the pension asset's gross value and its associated deferred tax as two separate Balance Sheet items. The pension's economic net value is therefore its gross value less the calculated deferred tax liabilities. The tax affects net wealth before realization but is only settled upon the modeled pension withdrawal.

What happens when my private pension is paid out?

At the selected realization age, Neptoon settles the accumulated deferred pension taxes and transfers the pension's calculated net proceeds to the Free Investment Portfolio. The pension asset and its associated deferred tax liabilities are simultaneously removed from the Balance Sheet. Realization therefore represents a transfer of existing economic wealth from private pension savings to the Free Investment Portfolio, not a new savings contribution. Deferred tax already recognized as a liability must not reduce net wealth a second time.

What if my private pension is realized during Phase 1?

If the selected realization age falls before Economic Balance Day, the pension's net proceeds after settlement of deferred taxes are transferred to the Free Investment Portfolio in Phase 1. The proceeds then contribute to wealth accumulation and earn the expected after-tax return of your selected Phase 1 investment portfolio. Pension realization may therefore affect the calculated Economic Balance Day.

What if my private pension is realized during Phase 2?

If the pension is realized after Economic Balance Day, the net proceeds after deferred taxes are transferred to the Free Investment Portfolio in Phase 2. The proceeds then contribute to financing your desired consumption, other financial obligations and Legacy. Subsequent returns follow your selected Phase 2 investment portfolio rather than the pension asset's previous return assumption.

What if I do not select a pension withdrawal age?

If you do not specify a realization age, Neptoon assumes that the private pension remains a separate asset until the end of your selected lifetime. The pension's remaining net value after calculated deferred taxes is then included in your Legacy. The model does not automatically link pension realization to the public pension age or Economic Balance Day.

Is private pension the same as public pension and ATP?

No. Private pension is an accumulated financial asset with a calculated wealth value, expected return, possible ongoing contributions, deferred tax liabilities and an independent realization age. Public pension and ATP, by contrast, are treated as separate future pension income streams with their own starting dates. These three components must not be confused or counted twice.

Does Neptoon calculate the actual withdrawal rules for my pension scheme?

No. Neptoon uses a simplified financial model in which the private pension is realized at the selected age and the net proceeds are transferred to the Free Investment Portfolio. The model does not necessarily reflect actual statutory withdrawal ages, scheduled pension installment periods, lifetime annuity conditions or individual tax rules applicable to your pension scheme. The selected realization age and tax assumptions are therefore economic modeling assumptions.

How is my private pension paid out in Neptoon?

Neptoon models private pension as a single lump-sum payment at the realization age you select. The model does not calculate recurring installment payments, lifetime annuities or gradual pension withdrawals. Upon realization, accumulated deferred pension taxes are settled, and the entire net proceeds are transferred to the Free Investment Portfolio. The amount then becomes part of your selected investment portfolio for the current phase and can contribute to financing future consumption and Legacy.

Does Neptoon follow the actual payment rules for installment pensions and lifetime annuities?

No. Neptoon uses a simplified capital model in which the entire private pension wealth is realized at a single point in time. The selected realization age is independent of the public pension age and Economic Balance Day. The model does not simulate recurring payments from installment pension schemes or periodic lifetime annuity benefits. Lump-sum realization is a calculation assumption and may not be a withdrawal method permitted by the actual pension arrangement.
