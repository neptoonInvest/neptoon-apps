Taxes on investments and wealth

Understand mark-to-market taxation, realization-based taxation and deferred tax.

Does Neptoon account for taxes?

Yes. Neptoon applies two fundamental tax mechanisms: ongoing taxation of investment returns (mark-to-market taxation) and deferred taxation upon realization of assets (realization-based taxation). Under mark-to-market taxation, tax is incorporated directly into the expected net return. Under realization-based taxation, the asset's gross value is retained while the calculated deferred tax is recorded as a separate liability on the Balance Sheet. Both mechanisms affect total net wealth and the calculation of Economic Balance Day and Legacy.

What is the difference between mark-to-market taxation and realization-based taxation?

Under mark-to-market taxation, modeled investment returns are taxed continuously, regardless of whether the investment is sold. Neptoon incorporates this tax directly into the expected after-tax return, so no separate deferred tax liability accumulates. Under realization-based taxation, tax payments are deferred until the asset is sold or otherwise realized. The asset's full gross value remains on the Balance Sheet, while the calculated deferred tax accumulates as a separate liability.

How are my selected Free Investment Portfolios taxed in the two phases?

Neptoon assumes that the selected Free Investment Portfolios are subject to mark-to-market taxation in both Phase 1 – Wealth Building – and Phase 2 – Consumption. The selected capital income tax rate is therefore incorporated directly into the calculation of the portfolios' expected after-tax returns. The portfolios do not accumulate a separate deferred tax liability on their modeled returns. The same tax mechanism applies in both phases, although you may select different investment portfolios with different expected return assumptions.

Why don't mark-to-market taxed investments accumulate deferred tax?

Because Neptoon assumes that tax on ongoing investment returns has already been accounted for through a reduction in expected investment returns. The portfolio's future value is therefore calculated using after-tax returns. If the model also recorded a deferred tax liability on the same returns, the tax would be counted twice. Mark-to-market taxed portfolios therefore have no separately accumulated tax liability on their modeled returns.

What does deferred tax mean?

Deferred tax is a calculated future tax liability associated with an asset that has not yet been paid. Under realization-based taxation, Neptoon retains the asset's full gross value on the asset side of the Balance Sheet and records the deferred tax separately on the liability side. Net wealth therefore already reflects the economic tax obligation, even though the actual tax payment only occurs upon realization.

How is the economic value of a realization-taxed asset calculated?

Neptoon distinguishes between an asset's gross value and its net value after deferred tax. For example, an asset may have a calculated gross value of DKK 2,000,000 and a deferred tax liability of DKK 300,000. Its economic net value is then DKK 1,700,000. The asset remains recorded at DKK 2,000,000 on the asset side, while DKK 300,000 is recorded as a separate liability. This ensures that the asset's gross value and associated tax obligation are not combined into a single figure.

How are gains on real estate and private businesses taxed?

Neptoon can model realization-based taxation of nominal capital gains on real estate and private businesses using the selected tax assumptions. The asset's gross value develops until realization, while the calculated deferred tax is recorded as a separate liability. Upon sale, the tax liability is settled, and the asset's economic net value becomes available to finance the remaining Life Cycle. The model does not automatically account for every actual tax exemption or country-specific rule.

What happens to deferred tax when an asset is sold?

When an asset subject to realization-based taxation is sold, the calculated tax liability is settled. The asset's gross value and the associated deferred tax liability are removed from the Balance Sheet, while the net proceeds become part of the model's economic resources. Because the deferred tax was already recognized as a liability before the sale, the tax payment must not reduce net wealth a second time. Realization therefore primarily changes the form of the asset and the timing of the actual tax payment.

Why can the timing of an asset sale affect Economic Balance Day?

Because the asset's gross value and associated deferred tax liability may develop differently over time. Postponing realization can also defer the actual tax payment. This may affect the asset's future net value and the economic resources available at different points in the Life Cycle. Neptoon incorporates these effects into its overall calculations of wealth, Economic Balance Day and Legacy.

How does Neptoon handle taxes on private pensions?

Private pensions are treated separately from the Free Investment Portfolio. Neptoon can include an existing deferred tax liability upon pension withdrawal and separate taxation of subsequent nominal capital gains under the model's pension assumptions. Pension investment returns are assumed to be net of ongoing pension investment return tax, so this tax is not deducted again. Deferred taxation and ongoing investment return taxation are therefore two distinct tax components.

Can I change the tax assumptions?

Yes. Under My Settings, you can adjust the available tax rates for different asset types. This allows you to explore how changes in tax assumptions affect net wealth, Economic Balance Day and Legacy. However, you cannot freely change the underlying tax mechanism for each asset type. Neptoon applies its predefined structure for mark-to-market taxation and realization-based taxation.

Is deferred tax the same as an actual cash tax payment?

No. Deferred tax is an economic liability on the Balance Sheet, but not necessarily a payment in the current month. The liability may accumulate over time while the asset's gross value remains part of your wealth. The actual payment takes place only upon the modeled realization. Neptoon therefore distinguishes between when a tax liability affects net wealth and when it affects available financial resources.

Does Neptoon calculate my actual taxes?

No. Neptoon uses simplified and explicit tax assumptions to illustrate the economic effects of taxation throughout life. The model does not calculate your personal tax return or reproduce every national tax rule, deduction, exemption or individual circumstance. The results are model calculations, not an actual personal tax assessment or individualized tax advice.

Does Neptoon account for spouses, cohabiting partners and special tax rules for couples?

No. Neptoon uses one consolidated economic Balance Sheet and is not designed to calculate the special taxation and benefit offset rules that apply to married or cohabiting couples. The model does not distinguish between two individuals' separate incomes, pension arrangements, tax circumstances or public benefits. Nor does it calculate transfers of tax deductions, individual income thresholds or reductions in public pension supplements resulting from a spouse's or partner's financial circumstances. You may include the household's combined assets and liabilities, but the results are based on simplified joint economic assumptions and do not constitute an individual tax or pension calculation for a couple.

Beregnes skat før eller efter investeringsomkostninger?

Neptoon beregner skat efter fradrag af de modellerede investeringsomkostninger. Det betyder, at det forventede bruttoafkast først reduceres med omkostningerne, hvorefter skatten beregnes af det resterende afkast. Modellen forudsætter dermed skattemæssig fradragsret for de relevante omkostninger. Dette kaldes et skatteskjold (tax shield), fordi omkostningerne reducerer det skattepligtige afkast og dermed den beregnede skat.

Hvordan påvirker skatteskjoldet mit forventede afkast?

Skatteskjoldet betyder, at en del af investeringsomkostningerne modsvares af en lavere beregnet skat. Hvis en portefølje eksempelvis har et forventet bruttoafkast på 8 %, omkostninger på 1 % og en skattesats på 30 %, bliver det skattepligtige afkast 7 %. Den beregnede skat bliver 2,1 %, og nettoafkastet efter omkostninger og skat bliver 4,9 %. Uden fradragsret for omkostningerne ville nettoafkastet være 4,6 %. Neptoon anvender denne økonomiske forudsætning i beregningen af afkast efter skat.

Is tax calculated before or after investment costs?

Neptoon calculates tax after deducting modeled investment costs. This means that the expected gross return is first reduced by investment costs, after which tax is calculated on the remaining return. The model therefore assumes that the relevant investment costs are tax-deductible. This is known as a tax shield, because deductible costs reduce taxable investment returns and consequently the calculated tax liability.

How does the tax shield affect my expected return?

The tax shield means that part of the investment costs is offset by a reduction in calculated taxes. For example, if a portfolio has an expected gross return of 8%, investment costs of 1% and a tax rate of 30%, the taxable return becomes 7%. The calculated tax is 2.1%, resulting in a net return after costs and taxes of 4.9%. Without tax deductibility of investment costs, the net return would be 4.6%. Neptoon applies this economic assumption when calculating expected after-tax returns.
