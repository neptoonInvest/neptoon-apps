Savings and building wealth

What should I enter as monthly savings?

Enter the amount you expect to contribute to your Free Investment Portfolio next month. In Neptoon, monthly savings represent an exogenous, positive contribution to your financial assets during Phase 1. This contribution increases your wealth in isolation, regardless of whether you also have loans or other financial obligations. Neptoon calculates interest, principal repayments and changes in your other assets and liabilities separately. You should therefore not automatically deduct loan payments from your savings contribution if those payments are already handled in the model's Balance Sheet.

Can you give me a practical example?

Imagine that you expect to invest DKK 5,000 per month in freely held securities while also having a mortgage. You enter DKK 5,000 as your monthly savings contribution. Neptoon adds this amount to your Free Investment Portfolio while separately calculating the mortgage interest, principal repayments and outstanding debt. Your savings contribution is a positive addition to wealth, while interest expenses may reduce your total net wealth over time. Principal repayments reduce outstanding debt and should therefore not be confused with an economic loss.

Should I include repayments on my mortgage and bank loans?

No. Monthly savings in this field refer exclusively to new contributions to your Free Investment Portfolio. Principal repayments on registered loans are handled separately in Neptoon's balance-sheet simulation. A repayment reduces both outstanding debt and the economic resources used to finance it, so it does not, by itself, change total net wealth. You should neither add principal repayments to your monthly savings nor necessarily reduce your savings contribution by them. The model examines how loan obligations can be financed through your total economic resources.

Must I be able to finance my loan repayments from my current income?

No. Neptoon is a Life Cycle model, not a traditional household budgeting tool. The model therefore does not require all future principal repayments to be financed directly from ongoing employment income. Loan obligations may also be financed through existing financial assets or other economic resources that become available later in life. Neptoon calculates the development of your assets and liabilities and examines whether your overall finances can support these obligations under the selected modeling assumptions. This is an economic calculation, not a guarantee of actual liquidity or access to credit.

Should I include my private pension contributions?

No. Monthly savings in this field represent contributions to the Free Investment Portfolio. Private pension arrangements are registered separately under Private Pension. Neptoon keeps freely invested assets and pension wealth separate so that their values, returns and future economic contributions are correctly reflected in the overall Life Cycle calculation.

I am retired. Should I enter public pension and ATP as monthly savings?

No. Public pension and ATP Lifelong Pension are registered separately in their respective fields. They represent economic income streams that Neptoon includes in the overall Life Cycle calculation. Entering them again as monthly savings could result in double counting. Monthly savings should only represent an independent expected contribution to the Free Investment Portfolio.

What if I am already retired and no longer saving?

You can enter DKK 0 as your monthly savings contribution. Neptoon will still use your existing wealth, registered assets and liabilities, pension payments and other economic resources to calculate your Life Cycle. You do not need positive monthly savings to reach Economic Balance Day or finance your desired consumption and Legacy.

What if I continue working and investing after retirement?

If you expect to continue contributing money to your Free Investment Portfolio, you can enter that amount as monthly savings. This also applies if you are already receiving pension payments. However, savings contributions continue only during Phase 1, until the calculated Economic Balance Day. Public pension, ATP and other registered economic inflows are handled separately and must not be counted twice.

What does annual growth in savings mean?

It is the expected annual nominal increase in your monthly contributions to the Free Investment Portfolio. For example, if you invest DKK 5,000 per month and select 3% annual growth, your expected monthly contribution after one year will be DKK 5,150. This assumption may reflect salary increases, inflation or a growing ability to save. It is not the same as investment returns, which are calculated separately on the portfolio's value.

How long do my monthly savings contributions continue?

Monthly savings contributions continue throughout Phase 1 – Wealth Building – until the calculated Economic Balance Day. Each month, the modeled savings amount is added to the Free Investment Portfolio. At the transition to Phase 2, these new savings contributions cease, and the model shifts to financing your desired consumption. Existing assets, loans, interest, principal repayments and other economic resources continue to be included in the calculation.

What if my Economic Balance Day is today?

If Neptoon calculates that your existing economic resources can already finance your desired consumption and Legacy, Phase 2 begins immediately. No further modeled monthly savings contributions are therefore made. This does not necessarily mean that you must stop working or investing, but rather that additional savings are not required to meet your selected financial objectives under the model's assumptions.
