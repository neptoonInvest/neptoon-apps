Public pensions and retirement income

Can I include both Danish public pension and ATP?

Yes. Neptoon treats Danish public pension (Folkepension) and ATP Lifelong Pension as two separate future income streams. Both can contribute to financing your future consumption. Their calculated economic present values are shown separately on your Balance Sheet. Unlike private pension savings, public pension and ATP are not capital assets that are realized and transferred as lump sums to the Free Investment Portfolio.

What amount should I enter for Danish public pension?

Enter your expected monthly public pension after tax, expressed in today's purchasing power. The amount should reflect both the basic pension and any supplementary pension benefits, including your own assessment of potential income-related reductions. You also specify the age at which you expect payments to begin. Neptoon does not independently calculate your individual entitlement to public pension, pension supplements or income-dependent public benefits.

How do I enter my ATP Lifelong Pension?

Enter your expected annual ATP pension payment after tax, expressed in today's money. If you are not yet receiving ATP, Neptoon assumes payments begin at your specified public pension age. If you already receive ATP, you indicate this separately, and the model includes payments from your current age. ATP is treated as a recurring income stream, not as private pension wealth realized in a single payment.

Where can I find information about my expected pension?

You can find information about Danish public pension and ATP through borger.dk, ATP and PensionsInfo. Check whether the stated pension amounts are before or after tax and whether they are expressed in today's money or future nominal amounts. In Neptoon, you should enter expected after-tax amounts in today's purchasing power. You must also account for any expected reductions in public pension supplements yourself.

How do public pension and ATP affect my Economic Balance Day?

Once pension payments begin, they contribute to financing your desired ongoing consumption. This reduces the amount that must be financed through the Free Investment Portfolio. Neptoon also includes expected pension payments that begin after Economic Balance Day when assessing the overall economic sustainability of your Life Cycle. These pensions may therefore affect the calculated Economic Balance Day even if payments start later.

Why can I already see public pension and ATP on my Balance Sheet today?

Because Neptoon presents an economic Balance Sheet rather than simply a traditional statement of your current assets and liabilities. An economic Balance Sheet also includes the present value of expected future income. The principle is similar to how equity analysts value companies: the economic value of a stock depends not only on a company's current assets but also on the value of its expected future earnings and cash flows. In the same way, Neptoon calculates the present value of your expected future public pension and ATP payments today. This does not mean the money is held in an account or can be withdrawn immediately. These are economic resources that only become available through future recurring pension payments. Their present values appear on the Balance Sheet, while actual payments enter the financing calculation when received. This prevents double counting.

How is the economic present value of my pensions calculated?

Neptoon calculates the present value of your remaining expected pension payments until your selected end-of-life age. Future payments are discounted using the model's real after-tax return during the consumption phase. The present value therefore depends on factors such as the pension amount, payment starting age, remaining lifetime and selected Phase 2 investment return. As pension payments are received, the number of remaining payments decreases and, all else being equal, so does their associated economic present value.

Does Neptoon assume that public pension and ATP increase over time?

Yes. Neptoon applies the same simplified adjustment assumption to both income streams: annual nominal growth equal to inflation plus 0.5 percentage points. At moderate inflation rates, this corresponds approximately to real growth of 0.5% per year. This is a modeling assumption, not a forecast of the actual adjustments to Danish public pension and ATP, which follow different rules.

Does Neptoon account for income-related reductions in public pension benefits?

No. Neptoon does not automatically calculate reductions in supplementary public pension benefits, the elderly supplement (ældrecheck) or other income-dependent public benefits. Private pension payments, investment income and the financial circumstances of a spouse or cohabiting partner may affect certain public supplements under applicable rules. You must therefore assess and enter a realistic expected pension amount after tax and any applicable reductions. Neptoon also does not calculate special pension and tax rules for married or cohabiting couples.

What is the difference between private pension and public pension or ATP?

Neptoon treats private pension as a separate capital asset with expected investment returns, possible ongoing contributions, deferred tax liabilities and a user-selected realization age. Upon realization, the entire net value is transferred as a single lump sum to the Free Investment Portfolio. Public pension and ATP, by contrast, are treated as recurring pension income received throughout the remaining lifetime. They do not follow the private pension's realization age and are not transferred as lump sums.

What happens to public pension and ATP at the end of my life?

Neptoon includes expected recurring pension payments until your selected end-of-life age. After that point, modeled payments cease and their remaining economic present value is set to zero. Public pension and ATP therefore do not enter your Legacy as separate capital assets. Any special survivor benefits, guarantees or individual pension entitlements are not calculated separately.
