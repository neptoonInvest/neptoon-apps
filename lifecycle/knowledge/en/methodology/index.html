What is the fundamental idea behind Neptoon Life Cycle?

Neptoon examines how your total economic resources can finance your desired standard of living throughout the rest of your life. You specify, among other things, your savings, desired monthly consumption, expected lifetime and Legacy. The model then calculates the earliest point at which you can transition from wealth building to consumption without violating your selected financial objectives. This point is called Economic Balance Day.

What is the difference between Phase 1 and Phase 2?

Phase 1 is the wealth-building phase, during which you regularly invest in your selected Free Investment Portfolio and accumulate economic wealth. Phase 2 is the consumption phase, during which your total economic resources finance your desired fixed monthly consumption and any remaining loan obligations. You select an investment portfolio for each phase, allowing expected returns and risk to reflect the two different economic situations.

What does Economic Balance Day mean?

Economic Balance Day is the earliest point at which the model calculates that your total economic resources are sufficient to finance your desired monthly consumption throughout the remainder of your selected lifetime while also meeting your Legacy target. The timing is calculated automatically from your economic assumptions. It is therefore an outcome of the model, not a retirement age you must select yourself.

How does Neptoon determine Economic Balance Day?

Neptoon calculates your finances month by month and examines possible transition dates from Phase 1 to Phase 2. For each candidate date, the model calculates the development of wealth, savings, consumption, loans, pensions and other economic resources until your selected end-of-life age. It identifies the earliest date at which your consumption can be financed while simultaneously satisfying your desired Legacy.

Why does Economic Balance Day change when I adjust my assumptions?

Because the timing is determined endogenously, meaning that it results from your other economic choices. Higher savings, greater wealth or lower desired consumption may, for example, bring Economic Balance Day forward. Conversely, higher consumption, lower expected returns or a larger Legacy target may postpone it. Neptoon recalculates these relationships whenever you change a relevant assumption.

Does Neptoon predict my financial future?

No. Neptoon calculates a conditional future economic path based on your inputs and the model's defined assumptions. The calculation illustrates how your finances would develop if those assumptions were realized. It is not a forecast of precisely what will happen in reality.

How reliable is the calculated Economic Balance Day?

The date is a mathematical result of the model's assumptions, not a guaranteed age of financial independence. Future investment returns, savings, pension circumstances and lifetime may develop differently from what is assumed. Economic Balance Day should therefore be used to explore financial possibilities and trade-offs rather than as a certain future date.

What happens if I already have sufficient wealth?

If your current economic resources are already sufficient to finance your desired consumption and Legacy, Economic Balance Day may occur at your current age. In that case, the model begins directly in Phase 2 without an additional wealth-building period. This does not mean that you must stop working, but rather that additional employment income is not required under the selected assumptions.

What if my financial objectives cannot be achieved?

If the model cannot identify a transition date at which your desired consumption and Legacy can be financed throughout your selected lifetime, Economic Balance Day cannot be established under the current assumptions. You can then explore the effects of lower consumption, higher savings, a revised Legacy target or different investment assumptions. The model does not create economic resources that are absent from the calculation.

Why does Neptoon use two different investment portfolios?

Wealth building and consumption represent two different economic situations. In Phase 1, you can select a portfolio suited to accumulating wealth. In Phase 2, you can select another portfolio suited to financing future consumption. Neptoon applies the respective portfolios' expected after-tax returns when calculating the two phases.

Does Neptoon calculate investment risk?

Yes. Neptoon estimates expected returns and risk, measured by standard deviation, for your selected portfolios in Phase 1 and Phase 2. These risk measures describe uncertainty surrounding investment returns, but the central Life Cycle calculation applies a fixed expected return within each economic scenario. The model therefore does not simulate every possible future market path.

Why does Neptoon Life Cycle not provide Monte Carlo simulations?

Neptoon Life Cycle models the household's entire lifetime economy, not exclusively an investment portfolio. The model includes listed investments, property, pensions, future inheritance, private businesses and other non-listed assets. Many of these assets lack observable market prices, reliable volatility estimates and statistically defensible correlations. A comprehensive Monte Carlo simulation would therefore require speculative probability distributions and could create an illusion of statistical precision. Neptoon instead uses deterministic economic life cycle model with stochastic overlay on investment portfolio combined with explicitly defined economic scenarios and financial-market uncertainty assumptions.

What do the three economic scenarios mean?

Neptoon uses a negative, a central and an optimistic economic scenario. The scenarios adjust investment return assumptions and illustrate how these changes affect wealth development and the timing of Economic Balance Day. They are alternative economic calculations, not statistical forecasts with assigned probabilities.

Are the earliest and latest Economic Balance Day ages guaranteed boundaries?

No. These ages illustrate the results under the selected economic scenarios. They are neither statistical confidence intervals nor guaranteed boundaries. In reality, Economic Balance Day may occur earlier, later or not at all, depending on actual economic developments.

What if investment returns fluctuate significantly from year to year?

The central Life Cycle calculation uses fixed expected returns and does not model the actual sequence of future market gains and losses. This means, among other things, that the risk of substantial losses early in Phase 2 is not calculated as a separate probability-based path. In reality, two investment paths with the same average return can produce very different outcomes when money is simultaneously withdrawn to finance consumption.

How is my other wealth included in the model?

Neptoon combines free investments, real estate, private pension savings, public pension entitlements, future values and debt into one economic Balance Sheet. Future economic resources may be included at a calculated value even before they are paid out or realized. When an asset's value is subsequently transferred to the Free Investment Portfolio, this is treated as a reallocation of existing economic wealth rather than a new increase in wealth.

Can I use the surplus Legacy for higher consumption instead?

Yes. You can increase your monthly consumption target or adjust your Legacy objective. Neptoon Life Cycle then recalculates the earliest feasible Financial Freedom Day. The model does not automatically increase consumption or force Legacy to equal its target: these remain your personal financial choices.

Can I use the surplus Legacy for higher consumption instead?

Yes. When Financial Freedom Day is already today (n* = 0), a substantial surplus above your target Legacy means that you may be able to finance significantly higher monthly consumption throughout the Using Wealth phase. Increasing consumption reduces the projected terminal Legacy, allowing you to enjoy more of your wealth during your lifetime. As long as the target Legacy remains achievable, Financial Freedom Day stays at today. Neptoon Life Cycle lets you explore this trade-off without automatically changing your personal objectives.

How does the model handle loans and principal repayments?

Neptoon calculates interest, principal repayments and outstanding debt separately. Principal repayments reduce debt and thereby increase equity, but they also require financing. They therefore do not create additional net wealth by themselves. Interest and other financing costs, by contrast, reduce total economic resources. This distinction applies in both phases and ensures that debt repayment is not counted as additional savings. All interest and principal repayments are financed through the Free Investment Portfolio in both Phase 1 and Phase 2, while outstanding debt and its gradual reduction are recorded separately on the economic Balance Sheet.

Does Neptoon account for uncertainty in my future income and savings?

No. Your monthly savings contributions and their annual growth follow the assumptions you enter. The model does not calculate probabilities of unemployment, career changes, salary increases or unexpected expenses. Significant expected changes can sometimes be approximated through Other Future Values, but this does not replace a detailed income forecast.

Does Neptoon account for uncertainty in pensions and inheritance?

No. Public pensions, private pension arrangements and expected inheritances follow the amounts, timing and growth assumptions specified in the model. Neptoon does not calculate the probability that pensions will change, expected inheritances will not materialize or future payments will be delayed. You can adjust your assumptions to explore the consequences.

Does Neptoon account for uncertainty in future taxes?

No. The model applies your selected tax rates and its defined taxation principles. It does not predict future tax reforms, changes in pension taxation or individual tax circumstances. The calculations should therefore be understood as results conditional on the tax assumptions applied.

How does my expected lifetime affect the calculation?

Your selected end-of-life age determines how long the model must finance your desired consumption and when your Legacy is calculated. A longer lifetime generally means additional months of consumption and may therefore postpone Economic Balance Day. Neptoon does not calculate the probability of how long you will actually live, but allows you to explore different lifetimes.

Why doesn't Neptoon include all my expenses and personal belongings?

Neptoon is a financial Life Cycle model, not a personal bookkeeping system. In Phase 1, ordinary consumption is already reflected in your decision about monthly savings. In Phase 2, you specify your desired consumption directly. The model therefore focuses on the economic resources and obligations needed to examine the transition between the two phases and the financing of your Legacy.

Can I rely on the calculations when the future is uncertain?

Neptoon applies a consistent set of economic and accounting principles to calculate the consequences of your choices. However, even a mathematically consistent model cannot eliminate uncertainty about future investment returns, income, taxes, pensions or lifetime. The results are therefore best suited to understanding economic relationships, testing alternative assumptions and making more informed decisions.
